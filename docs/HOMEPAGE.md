Visitors now arrive on the church homepage, rather than the foundation placeholder. The user selected [GNAAS KNUST](https://www.gnaasknustgh.org/index.html) as visual inspiration, supplied local photographs and requested an autoplay carousel, clear pagination, restrained motion, and a modern white navigation/light footer. src/app/page.tsx composes the public features/home entry point. HomePage remains server-rendered; only the photo carousel subscribes to browser state. Sections cover welcome/visitor actions, next-Sabbath contact guidance, church information, the PRD ministry inventory and sermon/event/news destinations. ## Carousel contract

- The manifest includes the selected user-supplied files in public/images, with neutral descriptive alt text. No person's name or office is inferred from a filename. Original files and their embedded attribution are preserved. Media-specific focal positions are metadata, rather than additional theme values.
- next/image supplies responsive optimized images. The first photo is preloaded and visible in server markup. At most the active and previous hero photographs are rendered, limiting simultaneous decoding; the previous layer remains until its replacement loads.
- Every fully loaded photo receives one seven-second CSS timeline shared by its active-dot progress and slow 6% zoom. Zoom direction alternates between photographs. The previous photo's current transform is captured before changing slides to prevent a scale jump during the shared 250ms crossfade. The title and actions enter once with a short, staggered fade and 12px rise. The user-approved lead-sized copy stays fixed as ?Come as you are and find a place to belong. Join us this Sabbath for?; only the final phrase cycles every four seconds between worship, Bible study and fellowship. The welcome sentence and Sabbath invitation occupy separate intentional lines. The word ?for? stays with the rotating ending to avoid an isolated animated word at narrow widths. No enlarged secondary headline is added. Overlapping grid cells reserve the longest phrase dimensions, so actions do not move. The carousel pause control and page visibility also pause this text cycle. Reduced motion shows worship statically. Screen readers receive one complete sentence without repeated live announcements.
- Rotation advances through the native progress-animation end event. There is no JavaScript interval to misinterpret optimized CSS 7s as 7 milliseconds. This replaces the rapid-cycling implementation. Active media must finish loading before progress begins; a failed image leaves usable welcome links and an accessible error message.
- Circular pagination has 48px targets and white dots/progress directly over the photograph, without a coloured backplate. The user-requested SDA Denim gradient rises from the bottom and replaces the charcoal overlay. The fade is bottom-anchored and ends transparently below the top of the photograph. Both are stationary during photo changes and do not intercept pointer events. Selection is identified with aria-current, an enlarged dot and a ring, not colour alone.
- Keyboard interaction pauses rotation. Pointer interaction with pagination pauses the timeline, while a photo itself remains viewable during autoplay. A compact icon-only pause/resume action sits inside the hero beside the pagination on larger screens and beneath it at narrow widths, preserving 48px targets. It has an accessible label and reduced-motion disabled state. There are no previous/next arrows or extra church-name eyebrow.
- Motion stops while the page is hidden. Reduced-motion preferences disable automatic rotation, progress and zoom, and zero the shared fade duration. Manual photo selection remains available. Browser preferences use useSyncExternalStore with a conservative server snapshot, avoiding cascading effect updates or hydration mismatch.

## Hero composition

The user requested a trial of a left-aligned photographic hero, a slight height increase and subtle gradient/text motion. The supplied KNUST screenshot confirms a left-aligned heading, copy and actions with bottom-centred pagination. CCC SDA uses this composition within the existing shared gutters; minimum height is 42rem on phones and 46rem from desktop, and grows naturally for enlarged text. The header is sticky and stays visible as the page scrolls. It is fully transparent with white navigation over the homepage hero. After the hero clears the header, its surface becomes solid white with dark navigation so later section text cannot show through. Other routes use solid white. There is no glass or backdrop blur. An IntersectionObserver tracks the hero boundary and follows header resizing. Copy reserves the measured header row height above it, and root anchor padding keeps destination headings below the sticky header. Dropdowns and mobile panels retain solid surfaces. Donate is the default blue header action; Plan a Visit is the default blue hero action, with a calendar icon, and Watch online has a play icon. The hero has no duplicate desktop Plan CTA. The live reference site was blocked; the user-supplied screenshot is the visual reference. CCC SDA retains its own typography, colours, content and controls.
